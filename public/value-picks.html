A value pick is flagged when SG Pools' de-vigged price on an outcome implies a lower probability than the tipster consensus does — i.e. positive expected value by that reference. The consensus is softer than a true sharp line, so this is "the crowd disagrees with the price", not a guaranteed edge. Record and ROI assume a flat 1-unit stake per pick and settle at the odd shown (the last price seen before kickoff). Picks are filtered by kickoff date in your local time. Full-time scores come from Flashscore (Forebet as a fallback); a pick with no score yet stays "open". Past performance says nothing about future results. Gamble responsibly.
