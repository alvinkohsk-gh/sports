A value pick is flagged when SG Pools' de-vigged price on an outcome implies a lower probability than the reference does — i.e. positive expected value by that reference. "All Tipsters" uses the full tipster consensus (softer than a true sharp line); "Statarea Only" uses the same EV method scored on statarea's picks alone, weighted by statarea's own graded accuracy. Either way this is "the crowd (or one site) disagrees with the price", not a guaranteed edge. Record and ROI assume a flat 1-unit stake per pick and settle at the odd shown (the last price seen before kickoff). Picks are filtered by kickoff date in your local time. Full-time scores come from Flashscore (Forebet as a fallback); a pick with no score yet stays "open". Past performance says nothing about future results. Gamble responsibly.
