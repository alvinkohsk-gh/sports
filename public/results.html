"Accuracy" here is simply: of the predictions a site made for matches that have since finished, how many were right — 1X2 (home/draw/away), Over/Under total goals, and Asian Handicap, scored separately. No tipster site publishes an Asian Handicap pick directly, so it's derived from whichever sites give a predicted correct score (currently Forebet and PredictZ) compared against Singapore Pools' actual handicap line for that match — a quarter line (e.g. -0.25, -0.75) is settled as two equal-stake bets on the adjacent half/whole lines, same as real money settlement, so a pick can win, half-win, push (line refunded — excluded from the win rate and shown as "P"), half-lose, or lose, not just win or lose. Only fixtures on the Singapore Pools board are counted, filtered by kickoff date (your local time) and, optionally, by league. 🏆 marks the most accurate site over the selected scope (min 10 graded picks). Full-time scores come from Flashscore (Forebet as a fallback); the rare match neither covers stays "result pending". The small "@N.NN" next to a result mark is the Singapore Pools price on that pick at the moment it was captured (the site's final pre-kickoff call) — not today's price; it's blank for older samples from before this was tracked, or a fixture with no recorded price that early. Past performance says nothing about future results. Gamble responsibly.
